## 2. What the evidence says
- **WealthWatcher** ([audit](V1_evidence/wealthwatcher-architecture.md)):
  - layer rules belong in the first commit, not retrofitted;
  - its "pure" engines weren't checked, and its hand-mirrored client types drifted;
  - its seeded test world is the part to copy, with invented data.
- **iOS** ([research](V1_evidence/ios-stack.md)):
  - SwiftUI + AVFoundation + Vision + Core ML is the path;
  - the model exports to Core ML today (21.4 MB FP16 with NMS, ~6 ms on an M1);
  - TestFlight needs the paid Apple program;
  - Xcode isn't installed yet.
- **Hub** ([research](V1_evidence/web-hub-stack.md)):
  - Vite + React + TS, served by the backend;
  - hub login by pairing, never the host's own session.
- **Realtime:** both researches agree on REST commands plus per-viewer snapshots; the pipe was the open point.
- **Cards** ([proxy spike](V1_evidence/cv-proxy-spike.md), synthetic boards, not #3):
  - **scale decides:** 16% of cards read at the production call, 90% on sharp frames when each card is ~120 px in the model input;
  - upside-down cards are fine; blur is the enemy;
  - our deck must be tested.
- **Spring Boot 3.4/3.5 are out of open-source support;** 4.1 is current ([README](V1_evidence/README.md)).

## 8. Not proven by this spec / risks
- **The table camera is unproven.** The proxy spike used synthetic boards; #3 decides phase 13.
- **Nothing on iOS has been compiled or run on a device.** On-device read rates at hand distance are unmeasured. The proxy spike suggests cards held close may be *above* the model's size range.
- **The mirror may drift on real nights** (mis-taps). How often a chip count is needed is only known after phase 10.
- **The SSE stream through a Cloudflare Tunnel** with heartbeats is untested end to end.
- **The PC must be on whenever Spade is used,** including during Apple's beta review for guest installs.
- **Win probability's cost** (exact enumeration pre-flop, multiway) is unmeasured.
- **Importing the 20 nights** depends on how faithfully names map to accounts (guests appear as `GAST #1`).
