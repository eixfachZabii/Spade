## Outcome
A TV or monitor at the table shows the live table in direction A, "The Oval". It pairs from the host's phone, never holds a hole card before showdown, and keeps running for a whole night on its own.

## Scope
### In
- **`hub/`:**
  - Vite 8, React 19, TypeScript 6.0;
  - TanStack Router (hash history) + Query;
  - Tailwind 4 over `tokens.css` (from phase 08's token build);
  - Inter self-hosted; Motion for one-off moments.
- **The table view, per DESIGN.md:**
  - the seat oval (the salvaged `positionUtils` maths), seats where players really sit;
  - drawn cards with one index;
  - pot and side pots in points; the board flip on `transform`;
  - **the turn pulse on a pseudo-element** (`transform`/`opacity`, never `box-shadow`);
  - the winner banner, which stays until the next hand;
  - the footer that says where the board came from and that any phone can fix it;
  - a connection strip that says why ("reconnecting since 21:14").
- **Pairing (ADR 0011), backend and app included:**
  - `POST /api/v1/hub/pairings` (rate-limited, a 10-minute code without look-alike characters, plus a QR);
  - approval from the host's player app (scan the QR or type the code);
  - the hub polls for its token;
  - `hub_device` stored hashed; the `HUB` role sees the public view only;
  - the app lists and revokes hubs.
- **The stream:**
  - one `useTableStream` hook: `EventSource` with the cookie;
  - the watchdog and backoff; reconnect on `online`;
  - reload when the backend's `appVersion` changes;
  - snapshots written into the Query cache, older versions dropped.
- **The kiosk:** Screen Wake Lock re-acquired; a "click to start" fullscreen fallback; dim when no hand has run for 15 minutes. Kiosk setup notes in USAGE.md.
- **Serving:** the hub is built into the backend's static resources (one origin, one process).
- **Tests:**
  - Vitest: the snapshot reducer, the card mapping over 52 cards, the oval geometry;
  - Playwright e2e against the backend's testworld scripted hand;
  - a screenshot test of the table view, with baselines on Linux CI;
  - the backend privacy test extended to the `HUB` principal.
- **The gate:** `scripts/gate.sh --hub`: `npm ci`, type-check, lint, Vitest, build, Playwright, the token drift check.

## Decisions
| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | **Dark only.** |
| D2 | **The hub never sends a command;** a phone deals and enters cards. |
| D3 | **The table view shows points;** euros appear only on the ledger page. |
| D4 | **e2e runs against the real backend's testworld,** not a fake server: the contract is real by then. |

## Done when
- The paired hub follows a scripted 6-seat night through the testworld:
  - board flips;
  - side pots;
  - the winner banner;
  - a reconnect mid-hand that resyncs without replaying flips.
- `verify-change` §3 with the hub as the viewer: no hole card reaches the hub before showdown, checked on the stream, not on screen.
- `scripts/gate.sh --hub` and CI are green.

## Risks
- **Pairing UX on a TV with no keyboard:** the QR path must work first try.
- **Long-running browser memory** over a night: run a 4-hour soak with the scripted driver.

## Not proven by this phase (expected)
- the real TV, box and Wi-Fi (10);
- OLED behaviour;
- 4K.
